Prototype status, privacy, sources and planning assumptions

Product and purchase status

Forgotten Stove is an alert-only V1 prototype. It does not switch off appliances, detect gas leaks, guarantee fire prevention or eliminate false alerts. Always supervise cooking and maintain required smoke alarms. Under-hood installation, flame-retardant materials, the approximately 85 dB buzzer target and complete product performance need validation. No delivery date, certification, award, university endorsement or insurance eligibility is claimed. Pricing is proposed; this page takes no payments.

Planning and unit economics

Calculator defaults are hypothetical and not evidence of product effectiveness. Use incident categories that do not overlap, and replace defaults with your own data. The proposed target of up to 47% hardware gross margin at $129 implies cost of goods of $68.37 or less: (129 − 68.37) ÷ 129 = 47%. This is an unverified target, not an achieved margin, and excludes marketing, payment processing, support, overhead and other expenses not included in cost of goods. The connected plan proposes SMS routing through Twilio. Fleet features and reporting are planned, not currently validated enterprise capabilities.
