A few good questions

Know what
you’re bringing home.

Does Forgotten Stove turn off the stove?

No. This V1 monitors heat and presence, sounds a local reminder and initiates remote alerts. Someone must respond and turn off the stove if necessary. It does not replace a smoke alarm, supervision or an automatic shutoff system.

What happens if the Wi-Fi goes down?

The powered device’s local sensing and buzzer are designed to operate independently of the cloud. Email, SMS and remote dashboard updates need an internet connection and working online services. The device requires its USB power supply; this design does not claim battery backup.

Will it work with my range hood and stove?

The sensing approach can be used above gas or electric cooking without connecting to the stove’s electrical circuit or gas line. Installation depends on hood material, available space, safe heat exposure and sensor coverage. Magnets require a suitable magnetic surface; other installations need an appropriate heat-resistant adhesive mount. Universal fit has not been established.

Will standing still trigger an alert?

mmWave radar can detect small movements that a basic motion detector may miss, including breathing-related motion. It helps recognize stationary presence, but cannot guarantee zero false alerts. Placement, distance, obstructions and other people in the detection area affect performance.

What does “cooling” mean in the dashboard?

Cooling is a thermal-trend display. It is not confirmation that the stove is off. A falling temperature does not by itself silence an unattended-hot-stove alarm. In the interactive preview, the Cooling scenario represents a cook returning while the thermal trend falls.

Is this a finished, certified safety product?

This is a university venture prototype being prepared for a Top Dog competition pitch at Louisiana Tech University. Product testing, installation validation, acoustic measurements and any applicable approvals remain future work. The university reference is project context, not an endorsement or award claim.

Forgotten Stove is an alert-only V1 prototype. It does not switch off appliances, detect gas leaks, guarantee fire prevention or eliminate false alerts. Always supervise cooking and maintain required smoke alarms. Under-hood installation, flame-retardant materials, the approximately 85 dB buzzer target and complete product performance need validation.
